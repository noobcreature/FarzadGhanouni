I am drawn to dynamic, problem-oriented environments where I can challenge conventional assumptions, identify overlooked connections, and discover new solutions. I also use AI tools to support research, organize information, and explore alternative approaches. My approach can be described as guerrilla-style: finding effective ways forward, even beyond conventional frameworks, through curiosity, continuous learning, and rigorous analysis, while maintaining disciplined execution.

My experience in B2B sales, interpreting, and coordinating and managing operational projects has taught me that getting things done requires more than analysis and problem-solving. Alongside this experience, my ongoing Master's studies in Business Coaching at the University of Tehran have broadened my perspective on understanding problems at a deeper level, appreciating different viewpoints, and using awareness-generating questions to improve interactions and collaboration.

I believe this combination of experience and capabilities can help me contribute effectively as an Assistant to a Chief Strategist.
